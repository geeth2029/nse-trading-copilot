## Confirmed rules (not open to reinterpretation)

- NSE-listed stocks only.
- Scanner selection requires weekly RSI strictly greater than 60 and membership in the approved large-cap classification.
- The 20-week SMA comes from the most recently completed weekly candle.
- `RPT = User Capital * 0.01`; entry price is the current reference share price; stop-loss is the specified SMA; `RPS = Entry Price - Stop-Loss`; quantity is `floor(RPT / RPS)` only if RPS is positive.
- If stop-loss is greater than or equal to entry price, show a warning and allow the user to continue, but never divide by zero or calculate a valid quantity from non-positive RPS.
- Pyramiding eligibility requires both `Weekly RSI > 60` and `Previous Entry Price <= Current 20-week SMA <= Previous Entry Price + 3`. The previous entry price is from the most recent entry row. Do not assume the action must be disabled.
- Each pyramid is a separate entry with its own preserved values and quantity; accumulated quantity is the sum of entry quantities.
- One exit applies to the entire accumulated position. Entry P&L is `(Exit Price - Entry Price) * Entry Quantity`; consolidated P&L is the sum of entry P&L. Do not include transaction costs.
- Archiving/removing a stock from the active portfolio must retain its trading history. A user must never access another user's records.
- No actual broker orders are placed.
